The Philosophy

We do not tame the fire.
We collaborate with it.

Mastering 1200-degree embers is an act of calculated chaos, demanding absolute precision and unyielding respect.

Ember was born from a singular obsession: the transformative power of smoke, ash, and heat. Chef Arjun Mehta spent a decade mastering the hearths of Lyon and the binchotan grills of Tokyo before bringing his craft to New Delhi.

Here, there are no shortcuts. Every ingredient is subjected to the primal elements—passing through open wood-fired hearths or clay tandoors—before being refined onto the plate. The kitchen operates as a theater of intense heat, while the dining room remains a sanctuary of absolute calm, shadow, and measured hospitality.
